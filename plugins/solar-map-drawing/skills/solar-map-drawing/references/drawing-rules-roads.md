## Roads

Draw streets **only at 0, 90, 180 or 270 degrees in the final output**. No diagonal/45-degree or curved street strokes. Keep the street on the correct side of the house/site and keep meaningful connections; its schematic path may differ from the customer drawing. Use horizontal/vertical legs for a bend and a compact orthogonal representation for a curve. This is a street-only style exception: do not rotate, skew or simplify the building/roof/panel geometry, or change north, just to square a road.

Retain the solid near edge plus parallel dashed outer edge. In labeled/finished output center the name between the two edges, parallel to their horizontal/vertical direction; map-only omits the name. The `roads` helper normalizes the final direction to the nearest cardinal axis while retaining its center and length; layout fitting includes this normalized extent. It positions the name in the new lane instead of reusing an old diagonal-text position. Keep width schematic, choose the outer-offset sign away from the house and check the work zone. Small street lettering remains red below 10 pt. Do not infer another street or turn a side road into a bottom caption.
