## Trace without changing the site

For vectors, select reviewed source IDs from `manifest.json` / `path-atlas.png`. Preserve exact coordinates, curves and closures. Split paths that mix roofs, leaders, glyphs or sheet furniture, copying source commands with evidence. Separate the entire exterior perimeter from roof interiors so their weights remain correct.

For raster/sketch inputs, trace in displayed page coordinates with shared roof junction vertices. Preserve concavities, offsets, raw panel corners, stepped/missing cells and equipment attachment locations. The final rooftop rectangles use the common-size production rule below; the original trace remains the evidence. Regularize rough marks only enough to clarify intent and record the interpretation; do not claim survey accuracy. Ruled paper or photo perspective is not roof geometry. For hybrids, the rendered image is the visibility reference; PDF clipping/hidden layers may not appear in path extraction.

Every added element needs a source ID or `evidence`. Do not assume rectangles are panels, loops are buildings or interior marks are ridges. Preserve genuinely cropped outlines as open segments; do not close across a crop edge. Avoid global merging, minimum-area deletion and bounding-box replacement of tilted panels. Source traces remain unchanged internally.

Roof accuracy is a topology check: follow every perimeter corner, ridge, valley, dormer and shared junction against the source at high zoom. Do not sketch a plausible roof from its bounding box, merge an attached wing into a guessed outline, or change/delete roof paths to fit wording. Compare the fixed trace with the source before adding callouts. Preserve the underlying roof/perimeter paths at leader crossings and apply the white backing required by [Callouts](drawing-rules-callouts.md).

Use the customer-art layout: equipment rectangles on the actual map with leaders to the full equipment wording. **Do not introduce numbered equipment keys, legends, new enlarged details, duplicate overviews or schematic replacement footprints as fit shortcuts.** Existing source device numbers (such as INVERTER 1) remain part of their names. Reproduce source-supplied detail views when needed; creating a new inset or changing this annotation style requires an explicit user request. Refit within the ordered zone, place/wrap direct labels and review source-supported small type before reporting an unresolved fit. Keep each device on its confirmed structure and wall. Source clusters without distinct device outlines establish a wall/group and identities, not measured individual spacing; preserve that association/order and flag unresolved placement instead of inventing another wall or detail view.
