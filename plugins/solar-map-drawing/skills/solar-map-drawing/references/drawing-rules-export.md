## Clean export and focused verification

Preserve the [wrapped-text block rule](drawing-rules-production.md#typography): each multiline block exports as one `<text>` with its `<tspan>` lines intact. Flattening layout groups must not split or discard those lines.

Final artwork must be loose editable paths/text: **no groups, defs, clipping paths, masks or invisible helpers**. Bake translations. Individual text may retain a matrix for source-confirmed condensation or road-parallel lettering; preserve its physical height. Never transform map paths or their strokes this way. Draw shared/reversed/partially coincident strokes only once, retaining the stronger weight. Intentional overlaps are the equipment mounting edge on its wall (inside/outside devices may share it) and the editable process-colored backing behind each leader required by [Callouts](drawing-rules-callouts.md). Keep each backing immediately below its colored shaft and above crossed artwork; cleanup must preserve the pair and its stacking order. Other device overlaps need correction. Keep filled arrowheads and canonical north cutouts visible. Layout groups remain internal metadata. Review `export_cleanup` and the actual SVG.

For a new job, first run `placard_layout.py plan WORK/scene.json --output WORK/zone-plan.json`, then choose `layout.map_box` and label positions inside its work zone. Run `render WORK/scene.json --output DELIVERY --mode MODE`, or render `WORK/order.json` with the same mode. Always supply the selected mode rather than relying on a legacy scene default. Each map keeps its own planned physical frame. Only finished output draws its cutline, warning and footer; the other modes retain the work zone without exporting finishing objects. Default delivery is only the WO-named SVG; `output_filename` in the report is authoritative. Add `--preview WORK/preview.png` for internal review and `--diagnostics WORK/qa` for initial/changed geometry or unresolved source details. Reports stay `needs_visual_review` until checked.

For new full output, confirm nominal versus actual cutline dimensions, exact warning/operational wording, footer SKU-WO-item, requested holes and `zone_check`. Fit failures must be corrected within the ordered size; do not enlarge the cutline, hide overflow with a mask, drop details, or reduce stroke weights. For new geometry, follow the full perimeter and roof junctions against the source comparison/overlay; verify panel count/representation, roof clearance, device count, wall/freestanding association, operational wording/boundaries and north. Check flat main structure, fixed weights, plain rectangles, leader routes/tails/gaps, aligned text and multi-map labels/separation. Correct known errors and rerender. Record actual observations and unresolved source limits; a successful render is not approval.

Map-only and labeled scenes are not complete production placards. Prefer the measured ordered-size profile and reserved work zone for all modes. If manufacturing cut size is unknown, those two modes may use the source-confirmed nominal ordered dimensions as an explicitly identified editing canvas with a reviewed `layout.map_box` and `order_context`; do not invent a production profile, apply a plastic reduction to metal, or call that canvas a verified cutline. Unresolved finishing-only hole patterns remain deferred for map output and block finished output. This exception does not authorize guessing the ordered size or source site. For a callout-only revision, reuse the source-hash-matched scene/manifest; change text positions/routes only. Keep elements, fit, rotation, equipment targets, north and strokes identical. Use one focused preview and geometry/stroke equality check. Do not retrace, inspect the whole packet or regenerate full comparisons for that edit. New attachments require identity checks before reusing old geometry. Cache extraction once per unchanged source; do not reread unrelated customer examples each run.

Return an actual clickable **SVG download**, using the host's attachment mechanism or real desktop absolute output path, plus a brief note on unresolved details. Keep PNGs, comparisons, scenes and reports internal unless requested. A PDF renamed `.ai` is not a native Illustrator document.
