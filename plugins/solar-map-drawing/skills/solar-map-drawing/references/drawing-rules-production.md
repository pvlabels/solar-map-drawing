## Production drawing rules

| Artwork | Physical stroke |
| --- | --- |
| Building exterior | 2 pt |
| Interior roof and wall lines, including dividers between attached wings | 1 pt |
| Individual panels / schematic array paths | 1.25 pt |
| Equipment rectangles | 1.5 pt |
| Callout leaders | 0.75 pt |

Fit geometry uniformly into the planned physical work zone; do not scale physical strokes, type or arrows with the map. Cutlines use the measured cyan **0.001 pt** production convention; footer type is **5 pt** red. These are separate from the map stroke weights above. SVG dimensions and viewBox use one unit per point. Recheck weights if scaling in another application.

- **Rotation:** square the main structure to its nearest 0/45-degree orientation with one rigid rotation of the entire site. Keep relative building/array angles. Use shared `structure_id` values and `alignment.primary_structure` when known; otherwise the largest outline bounds are used. Do not independently snap or deform walls.
- **North:** use `assets/north-arrow.json` every time, normally a 34 pt needle with outlined left half, filled right half and upright N below. Supply `north_arrow` metadata instead of source compass paths. Compensate source bearing by the site rotation, then snap to the nearest 45 degrees only within +/-10 degrees. Keep exact compensation outside that tolerance and retain raw/displayed values internally. Explicit job corrections require `display_rotation_evidence`. Never invent a missing direction or copy another site's bearing.
- **Panels:** all rooftop panels on one map use **one common rectangular short/long size** and an invariant **1.25 pt** outline. Portrait/landscape orientations may swap that same pair of dimensions; they are not different module sizes. Mark roof-mounted modules `mount_surface: "roof"` (ground arrays use `"ground"`). The renderer also recognizes centers inside a complete building footprint. Preserve count, group membership, source centers and bearings; standardize the production rectangles, retaining the raw source corners internally. Apply needed clearance by reducing the common size for **all** rooftop modules together, never by individually squeezing threatened modules. Require 1 pt visible clearance from exterior, roof and interior-wall strokes; exact shared panel borders/corners are allowed, but overlapping module interiors and near-parallel doubled strokes are not. Do not invent extra panels to fill stepped cells. Ground-mounted arrays retain their source representation and existing roof-clearance behavior. `solar_field` is still reserved for genuinely schematic source rows/footprints without countable divisions. Flag a failed common-size fit instead of varying panel sizes or changing the roof.
- **Exterior versus interior walls:** use `building` only for the **2 pt exterior perimeter**, and `wall` for every **1 pt interior dividing wall**, including seams between attached building wings. Roof ridges/valleys remain `roof` at 1 pt. Split mixed source paths into exterior/interior portions; don't import an entire outline at 2 pt just because one portion bounds a roof section. Equipment rectangles stay 1.5 pt even when mounted on a 1 pt interior wall. An explicitly source-confirmed internal mounting wall uses `structure_id`, `wall_segment`, `mount_side` and `wall_inward` as needed; do not relocate the hardware to an unrelated exterior wall.
- **Equipment:** one plain **unfilled 1.5 pt rectangle per identified device**, replacing circles, fills, Xs and strikes. For building-mounted equipment put its mounting path edge exactly on the wall centerline, with zero stroke-width offset. Keep the source building, wall and along-wall location; explicitly indoor equipment stays on the inside face. Use `mount_structure`, `wall_segment` and `mount_side` to remove ambiguity, and `wall_inward` for a genuinely open perimeter. **Freestanding equipment stays at its source location** with `mount_side: "freestanding"`; do not invent a wall or move it to a house. Link equipment callouts using `target_element` and retain the original source `target` as evidence. Trim underlying interior strikes physically.
- **Meaningful distinctions:** retain source device names/counts, new/existing designators, room qualifiers, partial-shutdown labels, energized-area boundaries and operational map notes. Use a reviewed `dash_pt` boundary for meaningful dashes. Never infer shutdown behavior from an array color or generic equipment name. Keep source-confirmed site orientation labels and identifiers. Place the ordered product's warning and footer in their own zones; preserve source lower shutdown text and legends above the footer. TFS receives no added array/extra callouts unless supplied/requested by its current source.
